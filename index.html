Early access

Get Qu tester access

Initial testers need Meta's Tester role until Qu's permissions are publicly approved. They do not need access to Qu's code or App Secret.

App owner

Invite the tester

  1. Open Qu's App Roles page.
  2. Choose Add people → Tester.
  3. Enter the tester's Facebook account and send the invitation.
Tester

Accept and prepare

  1. Sign into Meta for Developers with the invited Facebook account.
  2. Accept the pending Qu invitation.
  3. Make sure that account manages a Facebook Page. For Instagram, link a professional account to that Page.
